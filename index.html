    About Me

    Hey there, I'm Quinn. I'm a fourth year student at Oregon State University studying Electrical and Computer Engineering. My background is in embedded microcontroller programming, but I'm also looking into analog circuitry design and radio frequency applications as well. I like simplicity. I enjoy learning about complex systems by building my own, stripped to the minimum required to work, then adding functionality only as needed. I'm a general-class amateur radio operator under the call sign KK7NQV.
